Privacy Policy — TaskFlow Helper

Last updated: 2026-09-30

TaskFlow Helper ("the extension") is a browser extension for authorized users to automate repetitive payout-processing actions on a specific trader dashboard (the "supported site"). This policy comprehensively explains what data it collects, how it's used, where it's stored, and every party it's shared with.

What the extension collects and why

  • Login credentials (email + password): entered in the extension and sent directly to our authentication provider (Supabase) to sign you in. They are not stored by the extension after sign-in.
  • Session token: returned on login and stored locally in your browser to keep you signed in. Refreshed as needed. Sent to Supabase to authenticate each request the extension makes on your behalf (see below).
  • Trader account identifier (username): the extension reads your username from the supported site's own authentication token to lock your account to a single identity (anti-sharing). Sent to and stored in Supabase for your account.
  • Device identifier: the extension generates a random identifier (not derived from hardware, IP, or browser fingerprint) and stores it locally. It's sent to Supabase to enforce a single active device per account and is stored there against your account.
  • Transaction/payout activity log: each time the extension accepts (or attempts and fails to accept) a payout on the supported site, it sends the payout ID, amount, outcome, failure reason (if any), and retry count to Supabase. Used to enforce your account's transaction limit and to keep an audit trail.
  • Payout payment details ("requisites"): as payouts appear on the supported site, the extension reads the payment details already shown there — payout ID, amount, status, payment method, and the associated payee's bank/payment account name, account number, and bank routing code (IFSC) — and sends them to Supabase for record-keeping and dispute resolution. This data describes the payment counterparties of transactions carried out through the supported site under your account; it is collected only because it is shown on payouts the extension processes there, not collected about you directly.
  • Extension version: sent with each authorization request so the operator can gate outdated builds. Not linked to any other identifying data beyond your account.
  • Settings and local activity log: limits, timers, and the recent activity feed shown in the popup are stored locally in your browser only; never uploaded.

Where data goes / who it's shared with

Supabase (our authentication and database backend) is the only party any of the data above is sent to — it is the extension's sole backend for sign-in, per-account configuration/binding, transaction/audit logging, and payout-requisite storage described above. Nothing is sent to any other third party. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or sale of data, and no remote code is loaded — all code ships in the extension package.

Data retention

Local data (session, settings, logs) stays in your browser until you clear it or uninstall the extension. Account, configuration, binding, transaction-audit, and payout-requisite records remain in Supabase until deleted by the operator or upon a deletion request (see Contact below).

Permissions

The extension requests only what it needs to function: local storage (settings, session, logs), access to the active tab to start/stop the automation, and network access to the supported site (to read payout data and submit accepts) and Supabase (authentication, configuration, and the logging described above).
