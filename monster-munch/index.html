Privacy Policy — Monster Munch

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Last updated: 16 October 2026

App: Monster Munch (Android package com.monstermunch.game) · Publisher: Chandrajeet · Contact: chandrajeetbij+monstermunch@gmail.com

Monster Munch is a feeding game for children aged 4–8. This app is designed for children, so protecting children's privacy is built into how it works. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. It is written to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children (GDPR-K), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP), and Google Play's Families policy.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal information from you or your child.
  • There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no chat, no social features, and no tracking or analytics.
  • The app does not use an advertising ID. The Android AD_ID permission is explicitly removed from the build.
  • Everything your child does — stars, stickers, hats, wallpapers, profiles, and minutes played — is stored only on your device. It never leaves the device and is never sent to us.
  • The only network use is for purchases, which are handled entirely by Google Play, always behind a parent gate.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • precise or coarse location;
  • photos, camera, microphone, or contacts;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or persistent cross-app identifiers;
  • usage analytics, event logs, crash reports, or behavioural profiles.

Profile nicknames. The game lets you create up to four in-game profiles. Profile names are chosen from a fixed list of playful nicknames; no real name is requested or required. These nicknames are saved only on the device.

Where game data is stored

All game progress is kept locally on your device:

  • in the app's local storage (localStorage in the embedded web view);
  • on Android, progress is also mirrored to the app's private on-device storage so it survives updates.

This data never leaves the device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you reset a profile or uninstall the app. You can reset a profile at any time in Parents' corner → Profiles → Reset progress.

Purchases

Monster Munch offers an optional subscription and a one-time purchase ("Monster Club"). All purchasing is:

  • handled by Google Play Billing — we never see or receive your payment card or billing details;
  • placed behind a parent gate — a grown-up must press and hold a button for 3 seconds and then type a number written in words before any purchase screen or price is shown. Your child never sees a price.

When you make or restore a purchase, Google processes that transaction and the related information under Google's own privacy policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. We receive only the signal that an entitlement is active, which is stored on the device to unlock content. We do not operate our own purchase server and do not store any purchase or payment data ourselves.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, DPDP)

This app is directed to children and is intended for the whole family.

  • Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing that requires us to obtain verifiable parental consent under COPPA, Article 8 GDPR, or Section 9 of India's DPDP. No personal data of a child is collected, used, or disclosed.
  • There is no behavioural advertising, no profiling of children, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • Purchases are gated so that only an adult can reach them.
  • If you believe any personal information of a child has somehow been collected through the app, contact us and we will investigate and delete it.

No sale or sharing of personal information

We do not sell or share personal information. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and similar US state laws, we have no personal information to disclose, correct, delete, or opt out of — because none is collected.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or social SDKs. The only third party involved in app operation is Google Play Billing, and only when a grown-up chooses to make or restore a purchase. The app bundles its fonts and synthesises its own sound, so it works fully offline apart from purchases.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control:

  • Reset a profile: Parents' corner → Profiles → Reset progress.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app.

We hold no copy of your data and therefore cannot access, export, or delete it on your behalf — there is nothing on our side to delete.

Changes to this policy

If we change the app so that this policy must change, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL before the change takes effect. Material changes affecting children's data would require a new app release and review.
