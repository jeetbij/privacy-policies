Privacy Policy — TaskFlow Helper

Last updated: 2026-08-29

TaskFlow Helper ("the extension") is a browser extension for authorized users to automate repetitive actions on a specific trader dashboard (the "supported site"). This policy explains what data it handles.

What the extension collects and why

  • Login credentials (email + password): entered in the extension and sent directly to our authentication provider (Supabase) to sign you in. They are not stored by the extension after sign-in.
  • Session token: returned on login and stored locally in your browser to keep you signed in.
  • Account identifier: the extension reads your username from the supported site's own authentication token to lock your account to a single identity (anti-sharing). Stored in Supabase for your account.
  • Settings and activity log: stored locally in your browser only; never uploaded.

Where data goes

Authentication and per-account configuration use Supabase. Nothing is sent to any other third party. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or sale of data.

Data retention

Local data (session, settings, logs) stays in your browser until you clear it or uninstall the extension. Account and configuration records remain in Supabase until deleted by the operator.

Permissions

The extension requests only what it needs: local storage, access to the active tab to start/stop the automation, and network access to the supported site and to Supabase (authentication).
